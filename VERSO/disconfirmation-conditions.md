# VERSO Falsifiability Conditions — Pre-Registration

**Date:** 2026-09-02

These conditions were articulated before the Cairn behavioral observation experiment or any other empirical test of the VERSO thesis. They are recorded here as a pre-registration commitment.

## Core Claim Under Test

Prompt injection is an unauthorized orientation transition — a span crossing from datum-space to instruction-space without a governed boundary. Position governs; content inspection is structurally insufficient.

## Disconfirmation Conditions

### 1. Completeness of Content Inspection

If a content-inspection defense can be demonstrated to catch injection reliably regardless of the payload's position in context, then lexical features are diagnostic and VERSO's positional framing is unnecessary machinery.

### 2. Content-Only Hijack

If an attack succeeds with no boundary crossing — the payload sitting in a properly governed instruction channel that still hijacks behavior through content alone — then orientation is not the operative mechanism.

### 3. Novelty Reduction

If the governance layer VERSO proposes reduces to provenance tagging under a different name, the novelty claim does not hold. VERSO must demonstrate that orientation governance does theoretical or practical work that provenance tracking alone cannot.

---

These conditions are deliberately written to be falsifiable by a single clean counterexample each. If any one holds, the corresponding VERSO claim requires revision or withdrawal.
